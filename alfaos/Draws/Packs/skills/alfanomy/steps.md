# Steps

- The assembly order is the order of the parts: bottom up, level by level. Parts with the same z form one step.
- Keep one idea per level, so each step adds a readable group (plinth, carcasses, worktop, wall units, fronts).
- Use set_layout steps to show the sequence. Each cell adds one step and lights the parts added in it.
- Check the order before you finish: a part that stands on another part comes after it.
