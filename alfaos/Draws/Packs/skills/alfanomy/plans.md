# Plan sets

Conventions seen in studio drawings, used as defaults Claude may change when the person asks. Design stage, not production planning: ca. values are allowed, a few mm do not matter, walls are crooked.

## Set structure
- Sheet: A3 landscape, one frame. One scale per sheet, written in the title block.
- Right column, full height: legend blocks on top (appliances, accessories, materials), title block at the bottom. Empty legend rows stay; empty means open.
- Fixed note under the legend: all dimensions to be checked on site, discrepancies reported, heights refer to the finished floor (OKFF).
- Status stamp, red, slanted, over the title block: VORABZUG or VORPLANUNG. No revision index; the date and the stamp carry the stage.
- Page order: cover or perspectives, plan, elevations A, B, C (one per run, island side, tall unit, bench), details, interior views.
- Perspectives are line drawings without dimensions and without scale.
- Name elevations by object (Zeile, Insel, Hochschrank) or by letter A, B, C. Variants: V1, V2, geöffnet, geschlossen.

## View referencing
- In the plan, mark each elevation with a filled triangle arrow and its letter, outside the body, tip toward the viewing direction. No section lines.
- Scale: kitchens 1:20 or 1:25, furniture 1:10, room overview 1:50.

## Dimensioning
- Whole mm, no unit; ca. values and tbd are allowed at design stage. Text small and mono, vertical text rotated 90 degrees.
- Ticks at 45 degrees on the chain ends, no arrowheads. Short extension lines with a small overrun.
- Usually up to 3 chain levels: modules, block total, wall total. Parts and total run parallel.
- Chains sit outside the body: widths below an elevation and outside a plan, heights as one chain from the finished floor on one side. Never inside the body.
- Height chain, base to top: plinth 100 (studio value, to be confirmed), carcass, worktop 20 or 40. Mark the worktop top with a small triangle and "APL OK".
- Add small end dimensions for side panels, gaps and fillers (19, 22, 30, 70). Keep them.
- Odd edge modules (364, 656, 779) absorb the wall deviation. Keep the usual module width elsewhere, put the remainder at the edge.
- Open points: write tbd or a ca. value.
- Survey values (room, wall, window) are lighter grey than planned ones. Planned values are strong.
- Do not dimension at design stage: handles, socket heights, plumbing, lights, niche internals, drawer heights. Do it only when the person asks.

## Elevation annotation
- Under each column one function text, centred below the width chain: "Auszug mit Müllsystem", "Tür mit Einlegeböden", "Geschirrspüler", "Apothekerauszug", "Blende". Several lines top to bottom.
- Appliance code inside the outline: KS fridge, GS dishwasher, BO oven, KF hob, TK freezer, KGK fridge-freezer, DA extractor.
- Front operation marks, dashed thin lines: X over a door or flap, a triangle or V over a drawer. The apex points toward the hinge (door) or the pull (drawer).
- Push-to-open is written as PTO next to the front.
- Interior views repeat the elevation with the fronts replaced by shelves, drawers and appliance outlines.

## Line work
- Three weights: strong outline and front joints, thin part edges and handles, lightest for surveyed room.
- Cut walls, ceiling and floor: flat light grey fill or 45 degree hatch. Show no shadows and no textures in line drawings.
- Hidden lines, ceiling beam, plinth setback: dashed.
- Dimensions in their own colour, separate from the geometry.
- Red only for the stamp and the dimensions. Magenta clouds with a short mono text mark open items.
- Green or blue only as a rare special mark (socket, water); explain it in the legend.

## Stage logic
- Early stage: perspectives, a rough plan, 3 to 6 overall dimensions, appliance names. Elevations without dimensions or texts.
- Next stage: full chains, function texts, opening marks, wall hatch, variants, legend filled, window and door in the plan.
- Default: one stage per sheet. Say in finish which stage you drew.
