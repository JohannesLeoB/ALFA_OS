# Materials

- Use only materials that get_state lists under materials or tints. When nothing fits, take the closest one and record the choice as an assumption.
- A material without a tint is reported by check as missingTints. Replace it or say so in the assumptions.
- The material is also the slot of a part for variants: one material per kind of part, the same material for the same role.
- Fill tone shows the three face tones, fill material shows the material tints. Use material fill for customer and presentation layouts.
- Never invent a hex tone for a material; tones come from the catalogue.
